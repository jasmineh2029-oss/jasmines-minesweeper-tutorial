How to Not Explode and Lose All Your Limbs in Minesweeper

But first of all, what is Minesweeper?

Minesweeper is a game where you must reveal and find the hidden "bombs" or "mines" in a grid based off the few you're given. Safe tiles have numbers and tell you how many mines surround that tile on a 3x3 square. If you press on a mine, you explode and lose the game! You can flag all the bombs that you know based off the tiles and whenever you start a game, you always start with a blank tile and build off that. This game was originally created by Robert Donner and Curt Johnson, Microsoft programmers, to help familiarize users with the mouse and teach them how to left and right click. Intended to be fun, this game blew up and became really popular, in a similar way that Solitare was created to teach users to "drag and drop". This interactive and educational game can be played on multiple platforms, such as Google Chrome, Minesweeper Online/Minesweeper.com, Microsoft Store, and even Steam!

Useful and Helpful Patterns for Gameplay

  • The 1-2-1 Pattern
  • The 1-2-2-1 Pattern
  • The 1-1 Pattern

In these patterns, they must be along a straight edge and cannot have hidden tiles interfering with them for the patterns to work. Because of logical decduction, the 1-2-1 pattern has two tiles on the edges but the middle touching the 2 will always be safe. For the 1-2-2-1 pattern, the two tiles touching the 2s will always be the bombs and the rest will be safe. And because of the tiles on the left of the 1-1 pattern, the third tile will be safe to touch. These patterns are especially helpful for a beginner as these are bound to pop up in your gameplay and it helps you understand the game a little more.

And now.. for some tips and tricks!

My most helpful tip would be to look for corners, that have empty spaces in the 3x3 grid and if there's a 1 in the corner, that corner is always a bomb and the surrounding tiles should be safe (just imagine everything in a 3x3 grid!) Also, only flag those areas you are very sure about, avoid guessing since this game isn't based off luck, but logic. Start in the corners or edges to ensure a smooth start and with practice, you can improve your pace! If you're confused, move to another area on the grid and continue there, and if you're still stuck, Google is your best friend! Remember to use patterns and make educated guesses. On the top bar, you can see how many flags there are, and this is really useful as you will see how many you have remaining, helping to detect the bombs easier.
